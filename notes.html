What is Porosity — and why does it matter?

When we zoom into a material, we see more than solid matter — we see the voids between structures, called pores. Porosity is the volume of these empty spaces relative to total volume. It governs absorption, permeability, and mechanical behavior, and it's typically measured with mercury intrusion porosimetry, gas permeability tests, or 3D imaging methods such as X-ray tomography.
