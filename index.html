About

Postdoctoral Researcher · Dept. of Dentistry · Université Paris Cité – CentraleSupélec

Postdoctoral researcher at Université Paris Cité, in collaboration with the LMPS laboratory at CentraleSupélec, Paris. Ph.D. in materials science with experience in multimodal and multiscale characterization of materials.

Current project: characterizing pathological tissues at different scales to understand the impact of disease on tissue physicochemistry and microstructure — adapting lab techniques to clinical applications to support dental diagnosis and treatment decisions.

Also teaches practical courses in microstructure characterization (bone microstructure and mechanics) and supervises student projects.
